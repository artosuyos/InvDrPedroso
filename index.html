RECEPTION VENUE

Espacio Verde Resort

"Where every stay is unique"

A peaceful getaway for nature lovers, Espacio Verde Resort is a premier luxury eco-friendly hotel resort. It offers quality accommodations and delectable dining options nestled in a green paradise amidst Roxas City. It is with great joy that Marvie and Art have chosen this remarkable destination to celebrate the reception of their wedding, where family and friends will gather to share in the beginning of their forever.

The wedding reception for Marvie and Art's Wedding will be held at the majestic El Comedor Hall, an elegant banquet venue overlooking the resort's private lagoons, offering a stunning backdrop of water lilies and lush tropical landscaping—creating the perfect setting for an unforgettable evening of love, laughter, and celebration.

CEREMONY VENUE

Sanctuary of the Sacred Heart of Jesus

"Tumandok Hill, Pueblo de Panay"

Perched on the scenic Tumandok Hill in Pueblo de Panay Township, the Sacred Heart of Jesus Shrine offers a tranquil and breathtaking sanctuary dedicated to prayer, reflection, and the celebration of holy sacraments.

Renowned for its awe-inspiring 132-foot (40-meter) monument of Jesus Christ—the tallest shrine of Jesus in the Philippines—this sacred landmark stands as a majestic symbol of faith overlooking Roxas City, Capiz. Its serene atmosphere, panoramic views, and profound spiritual presence create a truly extraordinary setting for a wedding ceremony.

In this holy and magnificent sanctuary, Marvie & Art’s wedding will be celebrated in a place where love, faith, and God’s blessings beautifully come together, making their special day even more meaningful and unforgettable.

Tumandok Hill, Pueblo de Panay Township, Roxas City, Capiz
